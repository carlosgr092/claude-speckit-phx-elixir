# Lean preset — /spx:specify

Produce a minimal specification. Keep ONLY the mandatory sections:

- A one-paragraph overview (the WHAT and WHY).
- User stories: title + a single acceptance line each (priorities optional).
- Functional requirements as `FR-###` bullets.
- 2-4 measurable, technology-agnostic success criteria.

Skip: exhaustive edge-case enumeration, the assumptions block, and the
requirements-quality checklist iteration loop. Record at most one open question
inline; do not chase `[NEEDS CLARIFICATION]`. Straight quotes only, WHAT-not-HOW.
